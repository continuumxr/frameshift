# ERRATA-001 — corrections to the documents already relayed

*Edited for publication: names and links removed or paraphrased.*

**To:** the incoming review chair.
**Issued:** 2026-10-07, after the programme plan (withheld), `PROJECT_SETUP.md`
and `CHAIR_DIRECTIVE.md` were relayed.

Two of those documents carried a claim that the **previous** chair had already
rejected. The rejection predates the relay; the correction did not reach the
files before they were sent. This notice supersedes them on that point.

---

## 1. WITHDRAWN — the recovery guarantee

**As relayed**, the programme plan (withheld) §2 and `PROJECT_SETUP.md` §0.1
stated that any kernel that fails to boot on the device of an earlier, shelved
programme is recoverable. *[paraphrased for publication]*

**Both are withdrawn.** Previous chair's ruling, 2026-10-07: the claim is too
broad.

**What is not established:** that recovery is guaranteed for an *arbitrary*
bad image. Two successful recoveries are not a proof covering every case, and
recovery from an arbitrary bad image has never been tested. The claim also
does not cover the boot chain, which is never written.

**Why this matters to your role rather than being a detail:** the generalised
form is what justifies treating this project as cheaply iterative. If it is
only **[C]**, then the first few kernel flashes carry more risk than the plan
assumes, and the sequencing of Stage 1 is a legitimate thing for you to
challenge.

**Note:** `CHAIR_DIRECTIVE.md` was correct as relayed. It cites this exact
claim in §3 as the worked example of inference dressed as derivation, and its
§6 handoff already marks it withdrawn. Nothing in the directive changes.

## 2. The same error, one layer up

The withdrawn claim survived into two documents **after** it had been rejected
and corrected elsewhere — in a project whose own directive lists "a
retraction's replacement deserves the same scrutiny as the original" as a
standing failure pattern.

Recorded because it is the honest version of what happened, and because a
correction that is applied in some places and not others is worse than one
applied nowhere: it leaves the project believing the matter is settled.
